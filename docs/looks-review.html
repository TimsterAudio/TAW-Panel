TAW Panel · review sheet · version 2 · 27 October 2026

Panel looks beyond the hi-fi

The shared panel in three families: Apps, Audio and Utilities & Fun. Below are the App look as Isobar and Waypoint have it, the four audio apps redrawn in it, and the six looks for the utilities. Each look's window is 600 × 300, a little bigger than the Net Meter's 420 × 180, and can show the Net Meter or the app that look suits best.

Decided on 27 October

  • The Net Meter moves to Glass.
  • Spool's name comes out of the panel's colour schemes. Spool was the starting point, not where the family is going.
  • Isobar's and Waypoint's look becomes the panel's App look, locked as it is.
  • The panel is organised as Apps, Audio and Utilities & Fun, and only Utilities & Fun gets the variety of looks.
  • No title bars where possible, and an option to turn them off everywhere else.

The Net Meter today, in the hi-fi look. The faceplate is calm and warm, but at gadget size the badge and buttons take about a quarter of the height, and the graphs get what's left. Glass, below, is where it goes next.

The App look, locked

Isobar and Waypoint as they are. In the panel they would share one App look; Waypoint's kinds and statuses stay in Waypoint. The window buttons are drawn in the corner, so neither needs the system title bar.

Audio, in the App look

The four separates on chart paper

What the Library, Player, Equaliser and Analyser would look like in Waypoint's look, at 920 × 520. Each keeps its own job and controls; the faceplate gives way to the page frame. The family switcher moves into the rail, the one big reading takes the place of the faceplate's display, and violet marks what's playing, chosen or measured. The Build switch at the top shows them in dark.

Utilities & Fun

Six looks to choose from

The family with the variety. Each utility offers the six and starts on the one that suits it; the Net Meter starts on Glass. None needs a title bar.

One set of colour roles

Every look fills in the same roles, taken from Isobar's palette. The panel's controls and graphs draw from the roles, so a new look is a palette, its typefaces, a corner radius and a display style. Each swatch is light on the left, dark on the right.

Fitting them into TAW-Panel

  • Two frames. The faceplate (the hi-fi and Box looks, the Net Meter, the clock) and the page, a side rail and content (Isobar, Waypoint, the Library in Folio).
  • New parts drawn from the roles: sparklines, scrolling graphs, status pills, lamps and flap figures, beside the knobs and faders already there.
  • Isobar and Waypoint pin TAW-Panel instead of each keeping its own copy of theme.rs and ui.rs; Waypoint's kinds and statuses stay in Waypoint.
  • Barlow is already shared. Isobar, Waypoint and the Split-Flap Clock all use it, so those three looks cost no new fonts.
  • Open question: the TAW name and the "Timster's Audio Works" badge on apps outside the audio family.
