For you to decide

  1. Which layout is the Player's default: Separates (artwork, display and list together), Now Playing (big artwork and controls, the list as a side panel), or both with a switch, plus Mini as a small always-on-top window?
  2. Which finishes ship first. A suggestion: Anodised and Dark Anodised as the default pair, then Spool, Cassette and Walnut.
  3. Whether the accent is fixed per finish, or chosen separately as in Spool (its seven colours are offered above).
  4. Whether Simple is the default view for new users.
  5. Whether the light and dark builds follow the computer's setting automatically, with a manual override.
  6. Naming: keep "TAW-Panel" for the new system and rename the current crate for the Box family, or the other way round.
